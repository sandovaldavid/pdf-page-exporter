Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

PDF Page Exporter creates a PDF from the pages the Google Drive or Google Docs viewer has already rendered in your active tab. This policy explains what data the extension handles, why it uses that data, and how long it keeps it.

1. Overview and scope

This policy applies to the PDF Page Exporter Chrome extension and to this public website. It does not apply to Google Drive, Google Docs, the documents you choose to export, or any other website. Google handles your Drive and Docs data under its own policies and terms.

The extension has one job: build the PDF you request from pages already visible in the viewer. It does not require an account or create one.

2. Data the extension handles

The extension handles four kinds of data.

  • Page images. When you choose Export PDF in the popup, the extension reads the page images already rendered in the active viewer tab. The images are processed in memory to assemble the PDF you requested. They are not transmitted, saved in extension storage, or kept after the export finishes.
  • Viewer address and title. When you open the popup, the extension reads the address and title of the active tab. It can do so only when that tab is on one of the Google viewer hosts listed below. It uses them to confirm that the page is a supported viewer and to suggest a file name. Neither value is stored or sent.
  • Preferences. Your export quality and automatic scroll setting are stored in chrome.storage.local. This storage belongs to your Chrome profile and stays on your device. The extension does not copy these preferences anywhere else.
  • Export progress. The status of an export in progress is stored in chrome.storage.session. It lets the progress screen recover if you close and reopen the popup. Chrome clears this session storage when the browser session ends.

3. What it does not do

  • It does not send document content to any server, including servers run by third parties.
  • It does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, or crash reporting.
  • It does not sell, share, or transfer data for advertising or any other purpose.
  • It does not read or modify other tabs, other websites, or files on your device.
  • It does not use chrome.storage.sync or set its own cookies.

4. Permissions and why

  • scripting: injects the code that reads rendered page images and assembles the PDF. It runs only after you choose Export PDF in the popup.
  • storage: saves your preferences in local storage and the current export progress in session storage, as described above.
  • https://drive.google.com/* and https://docs.google.com/*: allow access to the Google viewer, where the extension works. These permissions do not grant access to other sites.

The extension does not request the tabs, downloads, or <all_urls> permissions.

5. Limited use

Page images are used only to create the PDF you requested. They are not used for advertising, profiling, market research, or any purpose unrelated to that feature. No one reviews your data unless you consent to a review for a specific support case, the law requires it, or the data has been aggregated and anonymized.

6. Retention and deletion

Page images exist in memory only while an export is in progress. They are discarded when the export finishes or when you cancel it. Preferences in chrome.storage.local remain until you clear the extension's data or uninstall the extension. Export progress in chrome.storage.session is cleared when the browser session ends.

The finished PDF goes to your downloads folder. It stays there like any other file you download.

7. Third parties and hosting

The extension does not send document data to third parties. It works with the Google viewer already open in your tab.

This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as IP addresses when it serves the site, under GitHub's privacy statement. This site uses no analytics, cookies, or tracking scripts of its own.

Google Drive and Google Docs are governed by Google's terms and privacy policy. This policy does not change those terms.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when the extension or its data practices change. The current version is always on this page, with the date shown above. The extension has no account system or mailing list, so updates are published here.

9. Contact and scope

You can send questions or requests about this policy through the project issue tracker. The repository is public, so please do not include sensitive document details in an issue.

This policy covers PDF Page Exporter only. It does not govern the content of the documents you export, how you obtained them, or what you do with the resulting PDF. You remain responsible for making sure you have permission to access and use those documents.
